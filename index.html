Athena

My own Jarvis, and getting closer every week.

Athena is an assistant I hand things to. I tell her what's going on in plain words, typed or spoken through a Siri Shortcut, and she keeps track of all of it: plans, promises, goals. She runs my day in sentences, not lists: a brief at 6 AM, a nudge when a real gap opens, and a 30-second recap at night. Next, she learns to take a whole task and finish it on her own, like researching something and reporting back.

  • One message, sorted: an appointment goes on the calendar, a promise to someone gets a reminder, a goal gets a first step
  • Nudges when a real window opens in the day, not on a timer, with a daily limit so she never nags
  • Writes emails for me and sends them only after I say yes
  • A recap each night and a ten-minute plan every Sunday: what moved, what slipped, and this week's three focuses

Budget

A household budget that talks to the bank.

Bank accounts sync through Plaid and new transactions tidy themselves up: messy card-processor names become real merchants, rules file them into categories, and transfers, refunds and subscriptions are spotted on their own. Around that: budgets with a safe-to-spend number, savings goals with honest timelines, shared bills with the household, and a forecast of the months ahead.

  • Shared expenses: split bills, see who's paid, settle up, and invite people with a link
  • Budgets, savings goals and a month-by-month forecast with confidence bands, computed in SQL
  • Postgres row-level security as the real privacy boundary, with money kept in integer cents
